# 2026-10-21/22: Better PDFs from drawings (branch `wf/pdf`)

Idea 7 of `docs/sciforge/dev/cadcraft-lessons.md`, approved by Luca: the PDF that
DRAWING > EXPORT > Export PDF writes gets layers, searchable text, links and metadata.
Spec and limits: `docs/sciforge/parity/drawings.md` (Export PDF row, known gaps, facts).
No change to FreeCAD's own files (no core patch).

## In plain words (for Luca)

Before, the PDF of a drawing looked right but was "flat": the title block's words were
drawn as little shapes (you could not search for the part number or copy the title), the
file's properties said "Drawing001" and "FreeCAD TechDraw", and there was no way to hide
the hidden lines or the dimensions in a PDF viewer.

Now the same PDF (it looks exactly the same: the test compares the pictures pixel by
pixel) also has:

- **Layers** you can switch on and off in the viewer's layer panel (Acrobat, Firefox,
  Okular...): Visible Edges, Hidden Edges, Centerlines and Center Marks, Hatching and
  Section Lines, Dimensions and Annotations, Border and Title Block.
- **Searchable, copyable text** everywhere: the title block, leaders and symbols and the
  parts list now have invisible text laid over the drawn letters (the way scanned
  documents carry their recognised text), written with osifont (FreeCAD's drawing font),
  embedded in the file, only the letters used. Dimensions and notes already were text and
  are not doubled.
- **Clickable links** for any web address (https://..., www....) or mailto: in a note, a
  leader or a title block field.
- **Document properties** from the title block: title, author (Drawn By), subject (part
  number and description), keywords (part number, material, scale, size), "SciForge" as
  the program that made it.
- Several sheets stay one file, each page its own size.

How SciForge knows which line is which: TechDraw (FreeCAD's drawing module) prints the
sheet with Qt, which does not say what a line is. Just before printing, SciForge slips
tiny, almost invisible coloured dots into the drawing, one before each kind of thing
TechDraw draws in a view (hatching, edges, centre marks, section lines). The dots come out
in the PDF in drawing order, so everything after a dot is of the kind the dot announces.
SciForge then reads the PDF, takes the dots out and puts every line on its layer. If
anything in that goes wrong, you still get the PDF exactly as before, and the log says why.

## What was built

- `sciforge/drawing_pdf_objects.py` (new, pure): a small PDF reader / writer for the files
  Qt writes (values, content-stream operations with their exact bytes, whole files with a
  classic cross-reference table; anything else is refused with a sentence).
- `sciforge/drawing_pdf_font.py` (new, pure): TrueType reading (metrics, Unicode cmap
  formats 4 and 12, names), a subset that keeps glyph numbers (unused glyphs emptied,
  composite glyphs keep their parts, checksums redone), the PDF objects (Type0 /
  CIDFontType2 / FontDescriptor / FontFile2 / ToUnicode). The licence is respected: OS/2
  fsType "restricted" or "bitmap only" refuses embedding, "no subsetting" embeds the whole
  font. Without a usable font: Helvetica (standard, not embedded) and a log line.
- `sciforge/drawing_pdf_core.py` (new, pure): `marker_plan`, `layer_content` (markers out,
  every painted path, text object or image wrapped in `/OC /SF<layer> BDC ... EMC`, never
  across a q/Q; Qt's stray closepaths and value-less colour operators dropped),
  `edge_layer` (solid / hidden width dashed / other dash), `text_runs` (the page's own text
  read back with positions: fonts' ToUnicode and widths, CTM and text matrices), `overlay`
  (invisible text, render mode 3, stretched with Tz to the measured width, anchored like
  the SVG), `find_links`, `xmp`, `enhance` (the whole file: one set of OCGs for all pages,
  `/OCProperties` with an order, link annotations on their layer, Info and XMP, PDF 1.5;
  a PDF/A file keeps 1.4 and gets no layers), `summary` (for tests and logs).
- `sciforge/drawing_pdf_export.py` (new, FreeCAD GUI): the markers (a `QGraphicsItem`
  subclass with its own `type()`, alpha 1/255, 0.01 mm) as children of every view item
  (`data(0) == "QGIV"`) of the page's scene (`TechDrawGui.getSceneForPage`, else the
  tab's view), at the view's middle; printing with them (removed in `finally`); the
  outline texts from SciForge's data (`template_primitives`, `drawing_annot.to_dxf`
  through a recorder, `table_layout_of`) with Qt's width for the SVG's "sans-serif";
  metadata from the drawing's (first sheet's) title block; merge; rewrite, and on any
  error the printed PDF is kept and a warning logged. `KEEP_PRINTED` lets tests keep the
  PDF as printed.
- `drawing.py`: `export_pdf` goes through `drawing_pdf_export.export`; the old body is now
  `print_pdf` (TechDraw's print, nothing added); `sheet_size_of(page)`.
- `drawing_sheets.py`: `export_pdf` hands all (or the current) sheets to
  `drawing_pdf_export.export` (same temporary part files as before, removed afterwards).

## Findings (also in the parity doc's facts)

- Qt's PDF of a sheet (FreeCAD 1.1.4 / Qt 6.8.3): vector, PDF 1.4, fonts embedded as
  subsets with ToUnicode, but every SVG text (title block, zone letters, SciForge's
  symbols and parts list) is outlines; no layers, no links; Title = internal page name.
  Page sizes were already right (A3 1191 x 842 pt) and sheets were already merged.
- A marker under the template item crashes FreeCAD in the print (seen twice); markers on
  view items are fine and survive TechDraw's rebuild of the edges.
- TechDraw sets hidden edges' z to 60 and then overwrites it with 70: hidden and visible
  edges are interleaved, so edges are sorted by dash pattern and width. SciForge's centre
  marks come out dashed (12:3 at 0.18 mm), like hidden edges at 0.35 mm: the width tells
  them apart.
- A marker outside a view's extent moved its caption (TechDraw places the caption under
  `customChildrenBoundingRect`); at the view's origin nothing moves.
- Two prints of the same sheet can differ in a few anti-aliased pixels of SVG text; a
  print with markers and the next one without were pixel-identical.
- A projection group made by script: TechDraw draws its views unparented at the sheet's
  bottom-left corner (the parity doc already warned about groups); markers in all of them
  are added and removed without trouble; a view wholly off the sheet is not printed, so it
  has no markers (its things are simply absent).

## Decisions

- Layer names in Fusion's UI style (title case, "Center"), British "centre" in prose.
- Section lines, break lines and detail circles go with hatching ("Hatching and Section
  Lines"); captions, notes, balloons, symbols and the parts list with "Dimensions and
  Annotations".
- The page's white background belongs to "Border and Title Block" (it is printed with the
  template); turning that layer off still shows white paper in every viewer.
- Invisible text rather than replacing Qt's outlines: the look stays exactly TechDraw's.
- No new dependency: everything is pure Python plus what FreeCAD bundles (osifont).

## Limitations

- PDF/A output (a FreeCAD preference) gets no layers (PDF/A-1 forbids them).
- The invisible text's highlight is close to, not exactly on, the drawn letters (Qt's
  font for the SVG's "sans-serif" is measured, osifont's glyph shapes are used).
- SciForge cannot make sheets of different sizes yet; the export keeps each page's size
  (tested with an A4 sheet made by script).
- An edge a person formats as dashed at the hidden line width counts as hidden.
- Not tried in Acrobat itself (not available here); poppler (pdftoppm, pdftotext,
  pdfinfo, pdffonts) reads the files without a single syntax error, and layer visibility
  was checked by rendering with the default configuration changed.
